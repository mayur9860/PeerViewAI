# Avoiding Structural Drift

Structural drift occurs when content migrates to the wrong section. Each section has a clear purpose. Introduction: what and why. Related work: what others have done. Methods: how you did it. Results: what you found. Discussion: what it means.

A useful editing technique: after drafting, read each paragraph and ask "which section does this belong in?" Section boundaries should be clean but connected.
