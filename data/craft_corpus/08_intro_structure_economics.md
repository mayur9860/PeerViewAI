# Structuring an Economics Paper Introduction

Economics papers follow a relatively rigid introduction structure. The typical pattern is: motivation, research question, methodology preview, main findings, and contribution to literature.

The motivation should connect to a real-world phenomenon or policy question. Unlike CS papers, economics introductions often preview the main results in detail. This is not a spoiler. Readers want to know your conclusions before deciding whether to invest time in your methodology.

End the introduction with a road map paragraph. This convention is nearly universal in economics and its absence signals unfamiliarity with field norms.
