# Controlling Sentence Length

Long sentences are harder to parse and more likely to contain grammatical errors. A good target is 15-25 words per sentence on average. Sentences over 40 words should be rare.

Vary your sentence length deliberately. A short sentence after a long one creates emphasis. Read your prose aloud. If you run out of breath before a period, the sentence is too long.
