# Anticipating Reviewer Objections in CS Papers

Experienced authors write their discussion section as a preemptive response to likely reviewer critiques. Common objections in CS include: limited baselines, small datasets, lack of statistical significance tests, missing ablation studies, and unclear practical impact.

For each limitation you can anticipate, address it directly: acknowledge the limitation, explain why it does not invalidate your core contribution, and suggest how future work could address it.

The "threats to validity" subsection is your friend. Internal validity, external validity, and construct validity should each get a sentence or two.

Never pretend limitations do not exist. Reviewers respect honesty and will be far more critical of a paper that ignores obvious weaknesses than one that acknowledges them.
