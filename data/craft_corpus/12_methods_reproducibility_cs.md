# Reproducibility in CS Methods Sections

Reproducibility is a growing concern in computer science. Your methods section should enable reproduction by specifying: hardware, software, hyperparameters, training details, and evaluation protocol.

Random seeds should be reported and used consistently. If your results are seed-sensitive, report variance across multiple seeds rather than cherry-picking the best run.

Link to code repositories where possible. For benchmark comparisons, specify the exact benchmark version and evaluation metric.
