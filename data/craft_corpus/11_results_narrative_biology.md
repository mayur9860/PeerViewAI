# Building Narrative in Biology Results Sections

Biology results sections often suffer from data dumping, presenting number after number without narrative connective tissue. Every table and figure needs prose that tells the reader what to notice and why it matters.

Start each results subsection with a sentence stating the question that subsection addresses. After presenting data, add a bridging sentence that connects to the next result.

Use effect sizes alongside p-values. A result can be statistically significant but biologically trivial if the effect size is small. Report both and let the reader judge the practical significance.
