# Effective Citation Practices

Citations serve multiple purposes: acknowledging prior work, providing evidence for claims, and positioning your contribution within the literature. Each citation should serve at least one purpose.

Avoid citation dumps without any discussion. Balance recency with foundational works. Self-citation should be proportionate. Having 30% self-references raises concerns about insularity.
