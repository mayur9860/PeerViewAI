# Writing Explicit Contribution Statements

An explicit contribution statement removes all ambiguity about what your paper offers. Without one, reviewers must infer your contributions, and they may infer incorrectly.

Common patterns include: "We make three contributions. First,... Second,... Third,..." Both numbered and bulleted lists work well. The key is clarity and specificity.

Each contribution should pass the "so what" test. Contributions should be verifiable in the paper. Mismatches between claimed and delivered contributions are a major red flag for reviewers.
